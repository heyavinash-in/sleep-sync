How SleepSync Works

SleepSync is designed to help you create your perfect ambient environment. Whether you are trying to focus on work, relax after a long day, or fall asleep peacefully, surrounding yourself with the right acoustic atmosphere can drastically improve your mental state.

Create Your Perfect Ambient Environment

Use the sound library to blend multiple acoustic tracks together. Mix the deep rumble of a campfire with the soft pitter-patter of rain, or combine ocean waves with a howling wind. You have complete control over the individual volume of every element, allowing you to sculpt the exact frequency masking you need to block out distractions.

Intelligent Sleep Timer

Falling asleep with audio playing shouldn't mean being jolted awake when it abruptly stops. Our intelligent Sleep Timer gradually fades your mix into silence over the final 30 seconds of your session, ensuring your transition into deep sleep remains uninterrupted.
